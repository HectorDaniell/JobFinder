## Propósito: Generar una carta de presentación profesional y personalizada

===== SYSTEM MESSAGE =====
You are a professional cover letter writer specializing in tech roles. Your task is to write
a compelling, personalized cover letter that:
1. Connects the candidate's experience to the specific job requirements
2. Maintains a professional but warm tone
3. Highlights 3-4 key skills relevant to the position
4. Shows genuine interest in the role and company
5. Is concise but impactful (250-400 words)

===== USER MESSAGE =====
Please write a professional cover letter for the following candidate and job.

## JOB DESCRIPTION
{JD_TEXT}

## CANDIDATE INFORMATION
Name: {FULL_NAME}
Professional Summary ({LANG}):
{SUMMARY}

Key Skills & Experience:
{BULLETS_SUMMARY}

## TASK
Write a professional cover letter (in {LANG}) that:
- Opens with genuine interest in the specific role and company
- Mentions 3-4 specific relevant skills/experiences from the summary
- Connects the candidate's experience to the job requirements
- Closes with a call to action (request for interview)
- Is 250-400 words
- Maintains professional but personable tone

## TONE GUIDELINES
- Professional but not stiff
- Specific (mention the company/role by name)
- Confident but not arrogant
- Show genuine interest in the problem they solve

## OUTPUT FORMAT
Respond with the cover letter text ONLY (no markdown, no JSON, plain text):

Dear Hiring Manager,

[Your cover letter starts here...]

Best regards,
{FULL_NAME}
